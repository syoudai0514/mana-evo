## 5. Grass anti-duplication decisions

The six families intentionally occupy six different primary reads:

1. F001 — **horizontal defensive mass**.
2. F014 — **walking root + flower**.
3. F028 — **hanging / arcing vine line**.
4. F029 — **succulent column + radial bloom**.
5. F046 — **vertical ancient habitat tree**.
6. F080 — **monumental axial world tree**.

Highest-risk collisions and the explicit separation rules:

- **F001 vs F046:** both can reach tree mass. F001 stays low/broad/defensive with the leaf→carried-tree signal; F046 stays vertically trunk-led and habitat-like, with seed/cheek history.
- **F001 vs F080:** F001 is a family progression with a defensive body role; F080 is a single-stage, centered world-tree icon and must not inherit F001's creature anatomy.
- **F046 vs F080:** F046 is irregular old-growth habitat; F080 is more axial/monumental and iconic. Do not reuse the same trunk/canopy silhouette.
- **F014 vs F029:** both flower. F014's identity is roots + movement + spring; F029's identity is succulent + thorn + one large bloom. Flower hue alone can never be their differentiator.
- **F014 vs F028:** F014 remains compact/grounded; F028 is defined by long hanging/arching lines and negative space.
- **all families:** no shared horn, pointed-ear, wing, dragon, armored humanoid or generic quadruped template; no green aura as the shared grass formula.

Attribute-level differentiation gate at packet stage:

- [x] Defining silhouettes are six separate categories.
- [x] Palette distribution differs by body material and signature location, not only green hue.
- [x] No horn/wing/ear/tail/armor template is assigned across unrelated families.
- [x] VFX is deliberately minimized; anatomy/motif carries the grass read.
- [x] Personality and pose follow CURRENT family concepts.
- [x] Final roles are defense / spring-bringer / guide-path / bloom-princess / habitat-tree / world-tree, not six larger mascots.

## 6. Generation packets

Common production constraints for **every** packet:

- original ManaEvo creature; do not imitate a specific existing IP;
- ages 5-8, but do not flatten all expressions into uniform cuteness;
- primarily 2-4 heads tall where anatomy permits;
- front to slight three-quarter view, full body visible;
- transparent background preferred; clean white acceptable;
- no text, number, badge, UI, frame or scenery baked into art;
- coherent soft upper/front key light; material-specific response;
- clean readable outer silhouette, no mandatory heavy black outline;
- grass identity must survive with VFX conceptually removed;
- later repository target is `public/monsters/mNNN.webp`, WebP strictly under 1,000,000 bytes;
- generate/review the complete family together, never disconnected stages.
